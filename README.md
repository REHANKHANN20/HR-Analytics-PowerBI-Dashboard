# 📖 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges faced by modern organizations. High employee turnover increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, affects team performance, and impacts overall business growth.

This project presents an **interactive HR Analytics Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI** to help HR professionals and business leaders monitor workforce performance, analyze employee attrition, evaluate employee satisfaction, and make data-driven decisions.

Instead of simply visualizing HR data, this dashboard transforms raw employee records into actionable business intelligence by combining **Power Query (ETL)**, **Data Modeling**, **DAX**, and **interactive reporting**.

The solution enables HR teams to identify workforce trends, detect high-risk departments, improve employee retention strategies, and support better organizational planning.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often collect large volumes of employee data but struggle to convert it into meaningful business insights.

Without an effective HR analytics solution, decision-makers face challenges such as:

- Difficulty identifying departments with high employee turnover.
- Limited visibility into employee satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Inability to monitor workforce performance efficiently.
- Lack of data-driven retention strategies.
- Manual reporting that consumes significant time and effort.
- Delayed decision-making due to fragmented HR information.

This project addresses these challenges by providing a centralized dashboard that transforms workforce data into interactive, actionable insights.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to build an end-to-end HR Analytics solution that enables HR managers and business leaders to:

- Monitor workforce health through real-time KPIs.
- Track employee attrition across departments and job roles.
- Analyze workforce demographics and employee distribution.
- Evaluate employee satisfaction and performance metrics.
- Compare salary trends across different job levels.
- Identify high-risk workforce segments.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.
- Support strategic HR planning using interactive dashboards.
